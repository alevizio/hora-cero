Files:
  cascarudo.glb      Santiago Siri's model (about 45,000 triangles), its shape as made, with a skeleton for its six legs
                     and its occlusion and convexity baked into its vertex colours by python3 tools/art_pack.py
                     rig-creature (tools/creature_rig.py)
  cascarudo_lod.glb  a lighter copy of it for far views (about 13,500 triangles), decimated and rigged alike

To build the game without it, empty the slot's "file" and "lod" in ../manifest.json: a neutral stand-in shows.
