Files:
  juan_salvo.glb                          the blue-suit survivor (a Meshy AI biped on a 61-bone Mixamo skeleton,
                                          10,390 triangles in one mesh), made ready for the game by
                                          python3 tools/juan_suit.py <its original .glb>: in metres with Y up, a
                                          standing rest with the rifle across his chest in both hands, the rifle on a
                                          bone of its own (art/juan_salvo/source/rifle.glb), the clips Idle, Walk, Run
                                          and Jump. The original file is not in the repository.
  juan_salvo_suit_basecolor.png           its colour, normal and metal-roughness maps (2048, 1024 and 512 px)
  juan_salvo_suit_normal.png
  juan_salvo_suit_metallic_roughness.png
  juan_salvo_rifle_*_v4.png               the rifle's maps, from Ale's earlier Juan
  portrait.png                            his HUD portrait (768 x 768): the avatar that came with the model

To build the game without it, empty the slot's "file" and "portrait" in ../manifest.json: a neutral stand-in shows.
