Sources (as delivered, kept unchanged in art/vehicles/ at the repository root):
  colectivo60_lod1.glb               "Colectivo 60 - game model", lighter detail level (43,436 triangles)
  colectivo60_lettering.png          its lettering texture (2048 x 2048; also inside the GLB)
  models/07_microcar.glb             "Retro Vehicle Pack", Microcar 70 (a small saloon of the Fiat 600 kind)
  models/08_work_pickup.glb          "Retro Vehicle Pack", Work Pickup 79 (a light pickup of the Rastrojero kind)
  source/build_vehicles.py           the Retro Vehicle Pack's generator (Python + NumPy), unchanged
Of the Retro Vehicle Pack's eight cars only these two are used; the other six (designs of 1969-92) and the
detailed colectivo60.glb are not in the repository.

Made for Hora Cero on the friends' generator (credit: the Retro Vehicle Pack's build_vehicles.py, whose
Model, materials and conventions it uses; same permission):
  source/build_vehicles_1950s.py     six generic cars of the 1940s and 50s, written to models/:
    11_sedan_49      a big rounded sedan with separate wings, the rear wheels skirted, whitewall tyres
    12_taxi_49       the same as a Buenos Aires taxi: black, its roof yellow, a small lamp on it
    13_saloon_53     a smaller rounded European-style saloon with a sloping back
    14_pickup_51     a pickup with separate wings, running boards and a step-side bed
    15_van_51        a delivery van with the same front, its body a tall rounded box
    16_coupe_48      a fastback two-door coupé
  Generic shapes only: no maker's badge, emblem, name, logo or any real model's grille. Each with the small things
  of a car of the time (chrome window surrounds and side spears, grille bars and teeth, bumpers with overriders,
  headlamps with reflectors and lenses, small round tail lamps, door handles and gaps, bonnet seams and boot lids,
  a plain chrome mascot, wipers, a mirror, an aerial on some, running boards, tyres with a tread, hubcaps, dark
  wheel wells, a taxi's meter flag) and its inside (seats, a painted dashboard, an ivory steering wheel).

Game meshes in this folder (made by tools/vehicles.gd from the sources; run it to rebuild them):
  colectivo60_open.res (the rules' colectivo on Hipolito Yrigoyen: doors folded open, its doorway lit, its lit
    inside behind see-through glass)
  colectivo60_far.res (the lighter one, for the colectivos on the streets beyond the map)
  microcar.res, work_pickup.res, sedan49.res, taxi49.res, saloon53.res, pickup51.res, van51.res, coupe48.res
    (the far copies, for the city's streets)
  microcar_near.res, work_pickup_near.res, sedan49_near.res, taxi49_near.res, saloon53_near.res,
  pickup51_near.res, van51_near.res, coupe48_near.res
    (the near copies, for the cars parked in the plaza's streets: inside and fine details kept, glass see-through)
  colectivo60_lettering.png (the lettering texture, scaled to 1024 x 1024)
When a mesh is missing (a build without these models), the kit's own procedural car or colectivo stands in.

What was changed from the sources:
  - The three-pointed star badge on the colectivo's grille and the round badge on its bonnet were removed
    (no makes' badges in the game). Everything else of the colectivo is as made, its route boards
    ("CONSTITUCION 60 TIGRE HOTEL") and its lettering included; the operator's name on its sides,
    "Micro Omnibus Norte S.A.", is in the lettering texture.
  - The colectivo mirrored left to right, so its doors are on the right, the kerb side (lettering kept readable).
  - Each model simplified (meshoptimizer, through Godot's LOD generation; the lamps, plates, route boards and
    lettering kept apart so they survive), the cars' flat faces shaded smooth (creases kept), with a snow cap over
    it. Triangles, snow cap included: the rules' colectivo 24,620 (its inside kept, lit, a lining round it, its
    glass a second surface), the distant one 5,450 (no inside); the far copies of the cars (no inside, no fine
    details, one surface): microcar 2,950, work pickup 3,490, sedan 3,410, taxi 2,890, saloon 2,410,
    pickup 2,790, van 2,720, coupe 3,440; their near copies (inside and fine details kept, glass a second
    surface): microcar 7,190, work pickup 7,420, sedan 9,770, taxi 10,710, saloon 7,640, pickup 9,010,
    van 9,090, coupe 9,440.
  - Added: a snow cap over the faces that look up, which the game raises as the night's snow deepens; on the
    colectivo, a fileteado panel under the rear window (our own drawing, plaza_b.png); on every car, number
    plates drawn on its plate boxes: eight invented registrations in the manner of the time (black enamel, a cream
    rim, "CAPITAL", numbers like 48-217), in plaza_b.png, none of them a real number.
  - Colours and materials re-made in the game's shader (godot/shaders/plaza_vehicle.gdshader): the colectivo's
    yellow and black from its own materials; the cars painted in sober colours of the 1950s (black, dark green,
    navy, maroon, grey, cream; work colours on the pickups and the van), the taxis black and yellow.
