VEHICLE SOURCES (Hora Cero)

The models as delivered by Santiago Siri (the Línea 60 colectivo and its lettering, two cars of the Retro
Vehicle Pack and that pack's generator), and six cars of the 1940s and 50s made for Hora Cero on that generator
(source/build_vehicles_1950s.py and models/1*_*.glb). tools/vehicles.gd makes the game's meshes from them, in
godot/assets/vehicles/.
